This privacy policy applies to applications published by ZWV Labs, including **SpaceJelly** and **Weave 2.0**.

## Weave 2.0
Weave 2.0 is a Wear OS Watch Face Format watch face. It does **not** collect, store, or transmit personal data to ZWV Labs. Optional complications display on-device system data only.

## SpaceJelly
SpaceJelly may collect device information (such as IP address, usage screens, OS) and **location** to power launch-visibility features. It may use third-party services such as Google Play Services. See the hosted policy for full details.
