This privacy policy applies to mobile and Wear OS applications published by ZWV Labs (“Service Provider”), including SpaceJelly and Weave 2.0 (each an “Application”). Different Applications collect different data, as described below.

Weave 2.0 (Wear OS watch face)

Weave 2.0 is a Watch Face Format watch face. It is resource-only and does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to ZWV Labs.

  • No account, analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or location collection by the watch face.
  • Optional complications (battery, steps, weather, calendar, etc.) show data from Wear OS and other apps you already use. That data stays on your device and is not sent to ZWV Labs.
  • The watch face does not require network access for its own operation.

SpaceJelly (mobile app)

SpaceJelly may collect information when you download and use it, which may include:

  • Device Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Pages or screens visited, time and date, and time spent
  • Mobile operating system

SpaceJelly may use cookies, SDKs, or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by law, consent is obtained for non-essential tracking.

Location (SpaceJelly)

SpaceJelly collects device location to provide location-based features (for example nearby launch distances and sky orientation), improve the Application, and support related services. Location may be processed with third-party services that support Application functionality, subject to this policy and applicable law.

Third-party services (SpaceJelly)

SpaceJelly uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including Google Play Services.
